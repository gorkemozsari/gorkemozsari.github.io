# EnkiGames

Small games, made in Germany.

## Games

### Persona Taboo
A party game for a room full of phones. A secret word is described in the voice
of a hidden personality — guess both, and guess fast. Play alone against bots or
with friends on their own phones.

- [Privacy policy](https://gorkemozsari.github.io/enkigames-legal/)

### Angry Balls
A physics game. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

- [Privacy policy](https://gorkemozsari.github.io/enkigames-legal/angry-balls.html)
